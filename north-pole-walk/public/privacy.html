Stroll

Privacy

Stroll keeps as little as it can, and only to run the walk.

What we receive

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to show who is on your walk and to keep you signed in.

What we store

The miles you log, the walks you start or join, your goal and your unit preference, and cheers you give. That information is visible only to the people on your walk.

If you log miles with no signal, they wait on your phone until you're back online, then they're sent. Nothing else is stored on the phone except your sign-in and settings.

What we don't do

We do not sell, share or advertise with your information, and we do not use it for anything else. There is no tracking.

Deleting your account

Open the gear in the top corner of the app, then Delete my account. Everything about you is removed; a group walk you were on keeps going for the others. Or email orionsmaxfield@gmail.com and we will do it for you.
