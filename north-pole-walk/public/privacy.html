Privacy - The Walk to the North Pole

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture. We use them only to show who is on your team and to keep you signed in.

We store the miles you log and the teams you join. That information is visible only to members of your team.

We do not sell, share, or advertise with your information, and we do not use it for anything else.

To have your account and data deleted, email orionsmaxfield@gmail.com.
