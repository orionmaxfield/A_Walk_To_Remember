Privacy - Stroll

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture. We use them only to show who is on your walk and to keep you signed in.

We store the miles you log and the teams you join. That information is visible only to the people on your walk.

We do not sell, share, or advertise with your information, and we do not use it for anything else.

To delete your account and data, open the Team or Friends tab in the app and tap Delete my account. Or email orionsmaxfield@gmail.com and we will do it for you.
